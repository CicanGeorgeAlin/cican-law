# CCTV in Restrooms — Ireland First

A public-interest, source-led guide within CICAN LAW.

Live page (after GitHub Pages publishes the commit):
https://cicangeorgealin.github.io/cican-law/restroom-cctv/

## Purpose
Explain the legal and data-protection assessment required before a private-sector organisation considers CCTV in restroom facilities. Ireland is the first jurisdiction; EU-wide material is a framework, not a substitute for national research.

## Editorial rules
- No sales pitches, affiliate links, paid placements or referral commissions.
- No claim that CCTV in a toilet is automatically lawful.
- The Irish DPC describes the threshold for restroom CCTV as very high and advises avoiding areas with a high expectation of privacy, such as cubicles.
- Article 36 is statutory prior consultation where residual high risk remains; it is not a general CCTV licence or blanket approval.
- Business directory entries are neutral pointers to publicly described professional services, not endorsements or pro-bono promises.
- Verify each source and provider listing before relying on it. Update this guide when law or official guidance changes.
