# GLOBAL LAW — LANDING PAGE SPECIFICATION

## Visual identity

The GLOBAL LAW interface uses the established CICAN visual language:

- pure black or near-black background;
- bright white primary typography;
- restrained secondary typography;
- minimal visual noise;
- strong spacing;
- no unnecessary decorative elements;
- authoritative, archival atmosphere;
- mobile-first;
- fast-loading;
- accessible contrast.

The design must feel like a serious public legal archive, not a commercial law firm, news site or generic AI chatbot.

## Global search

The search console is the central interaction.

Placeholder examples:
- Search a law, article, case or legal term
- What does this law mean?
- Search by country or jurisdiction
- Check a legal claim

Search should eventually support:
- natural-language questions;
- exact legal terms;
- article/section numbers;
- legislation;
- cases;
- courts;
- institutions;
- countries;
- languages;
- dates.

## Citizen entry

### I DON'T UNDERSTAND THE LAW

A prominent entry point:

> Start with your question, not legal terminology.

Examples:
- I have a housing problem
- I want to understand my rights
- My employer has not paid me
- I want to understand a court decision
- What does this law mean?

The system then guides the citizen toward the relevant jurisdiction, legal area and authoritative sources.

## Legal pathways

### FIND THE LAW
Search directly.

### UNDERSTAND THE LAW

### CHECK A CLAIM
Trace a legal statement back to evidence.

### COMPARE COUNTRIES
Compare concepts without assuming equivalence.

### SEE WHAT CHANGED
View legal amendments, replacements, repeals and historical versions.

### FOLLOW THE CONNECTIONS
Explore the legal graph.

## Evidence banner

Every substantive page should reinforce:

**SOURCE FIRST**

> Original law → authoritative source → version/date → interpretation → explanation

## Trust and uncertainty

The homepage should communicate:

> If we cannot verify it, we will tell you.

Potential statuses:
- VERIFIED
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED
- REQUIRES INVESTIGATION
- SOURCE UNAVAILABLE

## Mission

### THE LAW BELONGS TO THE PUBLIC.

GLOBAL LAW exists to make the world's legal systems more findable, traceable, understandable and connected while preserving the authority of the original sources.

## Disclaimer

GLOBAL LAW is an educational and informational platform. It does not replace applicable law, official legal sources, qualified legal advice or legal representation.

## Design rule

Do not fill empty sections with invented statistics, fake activity, placeholder legal claims or fabricated country coverage.

A smaller truthful homepage is better than a larger misleading one.
