# Cross-Jurisdiction Comparison Standard

GLOBAL LAW compares legal systems without assuming equivalence.

## Relationship classes
DIRECT EQUIVALENT — substantially the same legal function and materially comparable structure.
FUNCTIONALLY SIMILAR — similar function with important structural differences.
PARTIALLY SIMILAR — meaningful overlap with material differences.
CONCEPTUALLY RELATED — related but not equivalent.
NO DIRECT EQUIVALENT — no reliable equivalent identified.
NOT COMPARABLE — structures differ too fundamentally.
UNKNOWN — evidence is insufficient.

Every serious comparison must identify jurisdictions, concepts or provisions, legal dates, function, material differences, sources, evidence state, and limitations.

Similar wording, numbering, terminology, or policy purpose is never sufficient proof of equivalence.
