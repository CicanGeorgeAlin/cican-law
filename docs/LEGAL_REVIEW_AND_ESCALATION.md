# GLOBAL LAW — LEGAL REVIEW AND ESCALATION

## Purpose

Not every legal object carries the same risk. Review effort must scale with legal importance and uncertainty.

## Risk tiers

### Tier 1 — Low risk
Basic metadata, source discovery and non-substantive navigation.

### Tier 2 — Moderate risk
Terminology, plain-language explanations, historical metadata and ordinary relationships.

### Tier 3 — High risk
Rights, duties, criminal provisions, immigration, constitutional matters, penalties, court interpretation, cross-border effects, potential conflicts and legal-status conclusions.

### Tier 4 — Critical
Material likely to affect immediate liberty, safety, major rights, criminal exposure, constitutional validity or a person's significant legal position.

## Escalation

Higher-risk objects require stronger primary-source evidence, explicit uncertainty, deeper Investigation Gate review and human/legal-expert review when available and warranted.

## Publication rule

AI-generated material must never bypass evidence and review requirements.

## Disagreement

If authoritative sources or interpretations conflict:
- preserve both;
- identify authority and date;
- do not manufacture reconciliation;
- present the unresolved issue;
- escalate for research.

## Emergency principle

When uncertainty could materially mislead a citizen, the system should stop and say that verification is required.
