# GLOBAL LAW — MASTER ROADMAP

## Mission

**THE WORLD'S LAWS, CONNECTED AND EXPLAINED.**

GLOBAL LAW is a source-first public legal knowledge infrastructure designed to make law findable, traceable, understandable and connected for ordinary citizens, researchers and legal professionals.

> **We do not rewrite the law. We do not replace the law. We improve the path to the law.**

The authoritative legal source remains the authority. GLOBAL LAW provides the map.

---

# 0. NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

These principles govern every phase.

1. **NEVER INVENT LAW.**
2. **PRIMARY SOURCE BEFORE SUMMARY.**
3. **ORIGINAL LAW BEFORE AI EXPLANATION.**
4. **PROVENANCE FOR EVERY VERIFIED CLAIM.**
5. **CURRENT LAW AND HISTORICAL LAW MUST BE SEPARATE AND TRACEABLE.**
6. **NO FALSE CROSS-JURISDICTION EQUIVALENCE.**
7. **UNCERTAINTY MUST BE VISIBLE.**
8. **LAW, COURT INTERPRETATION, ANALYSIS AND EXPLANATION MUST REMAIN DISTINCT.**
9. **AI IS AN ASSISTANT, NEVER THE LEGAL AUTHORITY.**
10. **PUBLIC CONTRIBUTIONS CAN REPORT PROBLEMS BUT CANNOT BY THEMSELVES CHANGE VERIFIED LAW.**
11. **LEGAL SOURCE RIGHTS, LICENCES AND ACCESS CONDITIONS MUST BE RECORDED.**
12. **EVERY IMPORTANT OBJECT MUST HAVE A STABLE INTERNAL ID.**
13. **QUALITY IS MEASURED BY VERIFICATION AND TRACEABILITY, NOT DOCUMENT COUNT.**
14. **DESIGN FOR LONG-TERM ARCHIVAL SURVIVAL.**
15. **CITIZEN ACCESSIBILITY IS A CORE REQUIREMENT, NOT A LATER FEATURE.**

# 30. MVP DEFINITION

The first public version must NOT attempt to contain the world's entire law.

The first meaningful MVP should prove the complete chain:

**Citizen question**
→ **jurisdiction**
→ **legal area**
→ **legal concept**
→ **actual provision**
→ **original source**
→ **plain-language explanation**
→ **rights/duties**
→ **court interpretation where available**
→ **related law**
→ **history**
→ **verification status**

If that chain works perfectly for a small number of jurisdictions, the architecture can scale.

---

# 31. ULTIMATE DEFINITION OF DONE

GLOBAL LAW is never truly “finished” because law continuously changes.

The project reaches maturity when:

- citizens can navigate law without needing legal terminology;
- lawyers and researchers can reach primary sources quickly;
- every verified claim has provenance;
- historical versions can be reconstructed;
- jurisdictions can be compared without false equivalence;
- legal terminology can be understood across languages;
- EU, regional and international relationships are visible;
- uncertainty is explicit;
- legal changes can be detected and reviewed;
- public reports improve investigation without becoming law themselves;
- the system can explain its evidence;
- the architecture can survive decades of legal change.

## Final principle

> **GLOBAL LAW is the map.**
>
> **The official legal sources remain the territory.**
>
> **The map must never pretend to be the territory.**
