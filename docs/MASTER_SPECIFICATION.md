# GLOBAL LAW — MASTER SPECIFICATION

## Mission
Build global legal knowledge infrastructure that preserves authoritative sources, maps their structure and history, connects legal concepts, compares jurisdictions carefully, and explains law in accessible language.

## Authority chain
Citizen question -> legal concept -> provision/instrument -> authoritative source -> version/status -> interpretation -> related law -> comparison.

## Evidence hierarchy
A — Primary authority. B — Official institutional material. C — Recognized legal databases. D — Academic research. E — Secondary commentary. F — GLOBAL LAW explanations. Level F is never the source of law.

## Evidence-first rule
No important legal proposition is VERIFIED without supporting evidence. Record source identity, authority level, precise locator, dates, language, version, verification date, and limitations.

## Jurisdiction model
Distinguish sovereign states, constituent countries, territories, dependencies, autonomous regions, partially recognized states, supranational systems, and international organizations.

## Legal objects
A legal object may be a constitution, treaty, act, code, regulation, decree, ordinance, statutory instrument, judgment, court, institution, or legal concept. Each receives a stable internal identifier and provenance record.

## Temporal law
Law is versioned data. Record publication, commencement, amendment, repeal, replacement, historical versions, current version, and verification dates where evidence permits. Support historical questions such as: What did this provision say in 2005?

## Relationships
Use typed relationships such as amends, repeals, replaces, implements, transposes, derives authority from, interpreted by, refers to, depends on, supplements, conflicts with, and relates to.

## Comparison
Classify relationships as DIRECT EQUIVALENT, FUNCTIONALLY SIMILAR, PARTIALLY SIMILAR, CONCEPTUALLY RELATED, NO DIRECT EQUIVALENT, NOT COMPARABLE, or UNKNOWN.

## Terminology
Preserve original term, language, jurisdiction, meaning, legal area, authoritative source, related terms, historical usage, translation status, and comparison notes.

## Multilingual integrity
Original legal language is primary. Identify official, authorized, unofficial, and AI-assisted translations. AI translation must never be presented as authoritative legal text.

## Citizen layer
Answer what the law is, where it comes from, who made it, whether it applies, when it applied, what it means, how courts interpret it, what it connects to, and how other jurisdictions handle the issue.

## AI governance
AI may summarize, explain, classify, assist discovery, identify possible relationships, and generate research questions. It must not fabricate authorities, citations, quotations, legal conclusions, or certainty.

## Quality
Measure verified legal objects, provenance completeness, temporal coverage, unresolved claims, source reliability, terminology coverage, relationship coverage, comparison confidence, and update freshness. Never optimize for document count alone.
