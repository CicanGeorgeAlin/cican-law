# GLOBAL LAW — PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATORS

GLOBAL LAW should not compete by trying to have the largest pile of legal documents.

It should compete on trust, traceability, understanding and connection.

## 1. Source-to-citizen chain

Question → jurisdiction → concept → provision → source → explanation → evidence.

## 2. Legal graph

Laws are connected to:
- amendments;
- repeals;
- replacements;
- cases;
- concepts;
- institutions;
- treaties;
- EU/regional systems;
- related provisions;
- other jurisdictions.

## 3. Time-aware law

Users can distinguish current law from historical versions and see when changes occurred.

## 4. Claim verification

Users can investigate legal statements rather than simply receiving AI answers.

## 5. Jurisdiction-aware terminology

The same word can have different legal meanings. GLOBAL LAW keeps jurisdiction and legal concept attached to terminology.

## 6. Citizen-first navigation

Users can begin with ordinary language and real-life situations.

## 7. Transparent uncertainty

Unknown is an explicit and legitimate result.

## 8. Institutional understanding

Citizens can discover not only what a rule says, but which institution has authority and what legal basis gives it that power.

## 9. Comparative law without false equivalence

The system distinguishes direct equivalents from functional similarities and non-comparable concepts.

## 10. Long-term legal memory

The platform is designed to preserve relationships and historical states rather than merely serving today's webpage.

## 11. AI with boundaries

AI helps search, summarize, organize, compare and explain. It never becomes the source of legal authority.

## 12. Public correction without public rewriting

Users can report suspected errors. Evidence determines verified changes.

## Core differentiator

> **GLOBAL LAW is not an AI that tells people what the law is. It is an evidence-backed map that helps people discover, understand and verify what the law says.**
