# GLOBAL LAW Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Status: IN PROGRESS

- repository governance
- master specification
- evidence hierarchy
- provenance model
- temporal model
- comparison standard
- Investigation Gate
- machine-readable schemas

## Phase 1 — European Union
- EU legal hierarchy
- primary law
- secondary law
- CJEU jurisprudence
- institutions
- authoritative source registry
- 27 Member State registry

## Phase 2 — Member State mapping
Build standardized legal profiles for all 27 Member States.

## Phase 3 — Deep models
Ireland and Romania become the first detailed end-to-end jurisdictions.

## Phase 4 — Terminology
Build multilingual legal terminology and concept mapping.

## Phase 5 — Comparison
Build evidence-backed cross-jurisdiction relationships.

## Phase 6 — Wider Europe
Expand beyond the EU.

## Phase 7 — Global
Progressively map all major national legal systems.

## Phase 8 — International law
UN, treaties, international courts, conventions, human-rights instruments, maritime law and other international layers.

## Phase 9 — Citizen platform
Search, country navigation, legal article pages, comparisons, timelines, source navigation, and plain-language explanations.

## Phase 10 — Continuous verification
Automated source monitoring, change detection, review queues, broken-link detection, and freshness indicators.

## Definition of success

The project succeeds when a citizen can move from a question to an understandable explanation and then directly to the authoritative legal source, while the system transparently shows uncertainty, history, and relationships.
