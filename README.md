# GLOBAL LAW

## The World's Laws, Connected and Explained

GLOBAL LAW is an evidence-first, source-based global legal knowledge project designed to make the world's legal systems findable, traceable, understandable, comparable, and historically navigable for ordinary citizens.

> THE LAW BELONGS TO THE PUBLIC.

### Core principles
- Never invent law.
- Source before summary.
- Original law before AI explanation.
- Uncertainty before guessing.
- Current law must be distinguished from historical law.
- Similar concepts must never be treated as equivalent without evidence.
- Every important legal claim must be traceable to a source.
- AI is an assistant to the archive, not the legal authority.
- Quality is measured by verification and provenance, not document count.

### Scope
GLOBAL LAW will progressively map constitutions, legislation, regulations, courts, jurisprudence, legal institutions, terminology, EU and regional legal systems, international law, treaties, historical versions, and relationships between legal concepts across jurisdictions.

The strategic foundation is the European Union and its 27 Member States, with Ireland and Romania as early deep-model jurisdictions.

### Repository
- docs/ — governing standards and methodology
- data/ — structured legal knowledge
- schemas/ — machine-readable data contracts
- eu/ — European Union legal layer
- countries/ — jurisdiction research
- international/ — international-law layer
- research/ — investigations and unresolved questions
- scripts/ — validation and quality control
- website/ — citizen-facing application

### Status vocabulary
Research state: VERIFIED, PARTIALLY VERIFIED, REQUIRES INVESTIGATION, SOURCE UNAVAILABLE.

Legal status: CURRENT, AMENDED, PARTIALLY REPEALED, REPEALED, SUPERSEDED, HISTORICAL, PROPOSED, UNKNOWN.

These states must never be conflated.

### Disclaimer
GLOBAL LAW is an educational and informational legal knowledge resource. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal representation, and does not replace professional legal advice. Applicable official law, judgments, treaties, and other primary authorities remain authoritative.

## Mission

LEAVE NO LAW UNMAPPED.

LEAVE NO CITIZEN UNABLE TO UNDERSTAND WHERE THE LAW COMES FROM.
