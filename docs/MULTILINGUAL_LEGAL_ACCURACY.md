# GLOBAL LAW — MULTILINGUAL LEGAL ACCURACY

## Principle

A translated legal word is not automatically the same legal concept.

## Model

Original term → language → jurisdiction → authoritative meaning → source → translation → legal concept → related concepts → comparison class.

## Translation statuses

- AUTHORITATIVE
- OFFICIAL
- RECOGNIZED
- HUMAN NON-AUTHORITATIVE
- MACHINE/AI
- UNVERIFIED

## Rules

1. Preserve original legal language whenever available.
2. Never replace original wording with an AI translation.
3. Clearly label translation status.
4. Preserve terms that have no reliable equivalent.
5. Warn about false friends and misleading literal translations.
6. Compare legal concepts, not just vocabulary.
7. Never infer legal equivalence solely from dictionary meaning.

## Citizen mode

Plain-language explanations may be translated for accessibility, but the original source and translation status remain visible.
