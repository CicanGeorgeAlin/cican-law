# Investigation Gate

Nothing becomes complete merely because it exists in the database.

## Gate levels
G0 DISCOVERED — object identified.
G1 SOURCE IDENTIFIED — plausible authoritative source located.
G2 SOURCE VERIFIED — authority and identity verified.
G3 LEGAL STATUS VERIFIED — dates, amendments, repeal and supersession assessed.
G4 CONTEXT VERIFIED — hierarchy, relationships, institutions and jurisprudence mapped sufficiently.
G5 CITIZEN READY — safe citizen-facing explanation with visible provenance.
G6 MAINTENANCE READY — monitoring and update requirements defined.

## Blocking conditions
Do not advance when identity, jurisdiction, source authority, current status, translation meaning, material amendment history, or critical citation remains unresolved.

## Completion
A jurisdiction may be called SUBSTANTIALLY MAPPED only after constitutional, legislative, judicial, administrative, terminology, regional or international, and source structures have documented verification.

Unresolved questions are first-class data and must be preserved.
