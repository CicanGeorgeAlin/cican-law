# Legal Status and Temporal Model

Legal status is time-dependent.

## Status
CURRENT
AMENDED
PARTIALLY REPEALED
REPEALED
SUPERSEDED
HISTORICAL
PROPOSED
UNKNOWN

Legal status describes the legal object. Research state describes how confidently GLOBAL LAW verified it. VERIFIED does not mean CURRENT.

## Dates
Where applicable store publication, enactment, commencement, amendment, repeal, expiry, replacement, and verification dates.

Historical text must never be reconstructed from memory. If unavailable, record the evidence gap.

Future or proposed law must never appear in current-law results without a clear status label.
